---
title: Organising a graph
sidebar_position: 9
description: Groups, comments, reroutes, copy and paste, alignment and wire styles.
---

- <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>G</kbd> draws a **group** around the selection. Dragging its header moves everything inside it.
- <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>M</kbd> adds a **comment**.
- Double-click a group title or a comment to edit it, click its color dot to recolor it, and drag its corner to resize it.
- Double-click a wire to add a **reroute** point, then drag it to route the wire around other nodes. Deleting a reroute keeps the wire connected.
- <kbd>F</kbd> frames the whole graph.
- <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>C</kbd>, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>X</kbd> and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>V</kbd> copy, cut and paste a selection — nodes, the wires between them, groups and comments — into any material or subgraph, even in another tab. Pasted items land under the pointer.
- The **Arrange** menu aligns the selection's edges or centres and distributes three or more items evenly. **Snap to Grid** makes dragged and nudged items land on the grid.
- The wire button in the graph header, beside the heatmap button, switches how wires are drawn; each click moves to the next style: **Curved**, **Straight**, **Angled** (a diagonal between short straight ends) and **Orthogonal** (right angles with rounded corners). The choice is remembered in this browser.
- The thumbnail button beside it sets how nodes show their output: **None** (no thumbnail, and shorter nodes for a large graph), **Plane** (the texture flat), **Sphere** or **Cube** (the texture wrapped on a small shape lit from the upper left, which shows how a surface reads in light). Curves are always shown flat. The choice is remembered, and is also under **Settings → Editor**.
